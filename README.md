# Aaron John Sabu

**Ph.D. Candidate · Roboticist**

*Perception · Estimation · Planning · Control*

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, and a researcher in the [Sensing & Robotics for Infrastructure Lab](https://sri-lab.seas.ucla.edu/). I develop perception, estimation, planning, and control methods for field robots, with a current emphasis on autonomous inspection of marine infrastructure.
